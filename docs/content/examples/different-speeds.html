--- title: "Different Speeds" description: "How to notate music at first, second, and third speeds" ---

Carnatic music often takes a phrase through several speeds. The tala stays the same, and each speed puts twice as many notes in a beat as the one before. A composition's first speed is whatever it starts at, often 1 or 2 notes per beat, so the numbers aren't fixed. Only the doubling is.

In the DSL, a speed is a \beatDuration. Double it for each speed. The examples below start at 1 note per beat, so they use 1, 2 and 4. A composition that starts at 2 notes per beat would use 2, 4 and 8.

First Speed

One note per beat, so an avartanam of Adi holds 8 notes:

\cycle("|4|2|2|") \beatDuration(1) \line("First Speed") Sw: S R G M P D N S. Sh: sa ri ga ma pa dha ni sa

Second Speed

Twice as many notes per beat: 16 in the same avartanam.

\cycle("|4|2|2|") \beatDuration(2) \line("Second Speed") Sw: S R G M P D N S. S. N D P M G R S Sh: sa ri ga ma pa dha ni sa sa ni dha pa ma ga ri sa

Third Speed

Twice again: 32 notes in the same avartanam.

\cycle("|4|2|2|") \beatDuration(4) \line("Third Speed") Sw: S R G M P D N S. S. N D P M G R S S R G M P D N S. S. N D P M G R S

All Three Together

Change \beatDuration between lines to move through the speeds within one notation:

\cycle("|4|2|2|") \beatDuration(1) \line("First Speed") Sw: S R G M P D N S. \beatDuration(2) \line("Second Speed") Sw: S R G M P D N S. S. N D P M G R S \beatDuration(4) \line("Third Speed") Sw: S R G M P D N S. S. N D P M G R S S R G M P D N S. S. N D P M G R S

Using Groups for Variety

Combine groups with beat duration for rhythmic variety:

\cycle("|4|2|2|") \beatDuration(4) \line("Mixed grouping") Sw: S R [G M] P [D N S.] R. [S. N D P] M G R S

Tips

  • Pick the first speed's \beatDuration from the composition, then double it for each speed after
  • Use \line with margin text to mark speed changes
  • Square brackets create subdivisions within a beat
  • Speed is separate from gati: tisram puts 3 notes in a beat, at any speed. See Tisram
