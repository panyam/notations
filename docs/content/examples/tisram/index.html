--- title: "Tisram Notation" description: "Working with 3 notes per beat (tisra gati)" ---

Tisram, or tisra gati, puts 3 notes in each beat. It is one of the five gatis, along with chatusram (4), khandam (5), misram (7) and sankeernam (9). The tala doesn't change: Adi still has 8 beats, and each beat is counted ta ki ta instead of ta ka di mi.

In the DSL, \beatDuration(3) sets 3 notes to a beat. Write each swara as its own note, separated by spaces. S R G is three notes, and SRG would be a single note named "SRG".

Tisram in Adi Tala

Eight beats of three notes each make 24 notes per avartanam:

\cycle("|4|2|2|") \beatDuration(3) S R G R G M G M P M P D P D N D N S. S. N D N D P

Tisram vs Chatusram

The same tala at 4 notes per beat and then at 3. Only \beatDuration changes:

\cycle("|4|2|2|") \beatDuration(4) \line("Chatusram (4 per beat)") S R G M R G M P G M P D M P D N P D N S. S. N D P N D P M D P M G \beatDuration(3) \line("Tisram (3 per beat)") S R G R G M G M P M P D P D N D N S. S. N D N D P

Tisram in Other Talas

Rupaka Tala (6 beats)

\cycle("|2|4|") \beatDuration(3) S R G R G M G M P M P D P D N D N S.

Misra Chapu (7 beats)

\cycle("|3|2|2|") \beatDuration(3) S R G R G M G M P M P D P D N D N S. S. , ,

Tisram Phrases

Descending

\cycle("|4|2|2|") \beatDuration(3) S. N D N D P D P M P M G M G R G R S S , , , , ,

Repeated groups

\cycle("|4|2|2|") \beatDuration(3) S R G S R G R G M R G M G M P G M P M P D M P D

Tisram with Gamakas

Gamakas go in front of the note they belong to, as anywhere else:

\cycle("|4|2|2|") \beatDuration(3) S R ~/ G R G ~ M G M P M P D P D N D N S. S. N D N D P

Practical Tips

  • Count each beat as ta ki ta while keeping the tala's claps and waves as usual
  • Practice with a metronome to feel 3 against the 4 of chatusram
  • Change \beatDuration between lines to move between gatis
  • Mark tisram sections with \line so readers see the change of gati
